## Coverage Boundary

This search establishes direct antecedents for multiway CKKS trees, hybrid CKKS/FHEW private-tree evaluation, CKKS encrypted-query LUTs, radix/discrete-CKKS comparison, single-level character-block LUT/radix arithmetic in Lattigo, and discrete-CKKS private-program execution with a direct private decision-tree benchmark. Within this dated 96-query corpus, no inspected primary work implements the exact Gao--Zheng triangle/A2B representation inside LCPDTE's non-interactive OBO/BSGS model-private evaluator. That is only a candidate exact-composition gap inside the recorded corpus. It is not a closest-work claim, a priority claim, or proof that no other work exists.
