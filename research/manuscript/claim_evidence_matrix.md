## Manuscript promotion rules

- A `supported` row may enter the manuscript only with the bounded wording in that row.
- A `partially supported` row may describe the verified layer and must keep the unverified layer out of the same sentence.
- A `hypothesis` row belongs in motivation, design rationale or an explicit research question, never in Results or the abstract's result slot.
- A `blocked` row stays as a named implementation/evidence gate. It becomes prose only after the gate produces a local artifact and this matrix is refreshed.
- Results, performance, security and superiority statements remain placeholders in the manuscript even when a functional test is green; they are filled from a pinned experiment artifact, not from a live workspace snapshot.
