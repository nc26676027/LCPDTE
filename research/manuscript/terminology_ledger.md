## Naming rules

1. Use one canonical term for one object. Do not introduce synonyms for stylistic variety.
2. Distinguish the published **LCPDTE baseline** from the local **integer-aware LCPDTE variants**.
3. Use mathematical notation in prose and code identifiers only when referring to implementation symbols.
4. Define an abbreviation once at first use, then use its canonical short form.
5. Keep proposed operators visibly separate from reproduced Gao–Zheng operators.
